A little clarity before you make time for yourself.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose your ritual, preferred date and time in our booking form. It opens a message in WhatsApp for you to send. Your appointment is confirmed when the salon replies. You can also call +91 98406 38981.

Can I book a Sunday visit?

Sunday opening hours are 10 AM to 4:30 PM. Monday to Saturday, the salon opens from 10 AM to 8:30 PM. Please message or call to confirm your appointment. Holiday hours may differ.

Are the menu prices the final quote?

The menu shows starting prices. Your final quote depends on your chosen treatment and bridal styling needs. We will confirm the selection and price with you during consultation.

What should I share for a bridal consultation?

Tell us your wedding date, muhurtham timing and venue. Share your saree or outfit, jewellery and the makeup references you like, so we can discuss the finish, draping and floral setting together.

Where is the parlour?

Find Abirami Natural Beauty Parlour at No. 1/11, Rice Mill Rd, Jalladiampet, Medavakkam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600100. Use the directions link below to plan your route.
