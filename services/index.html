A few things to know

Before
you visit.

Where is Abirami Natural Beauty Parlour?

Find us at No.1/11, Rice Mill Rd, Jalladiampet, Medavakkam, Chennai 600100. Use the directions link for your route.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is through WhatsApp. Send your name, the service you want to ask about and a preferred date. Your appointment is confirmed only after the salon replies. The booking link will appear when the verified number is added.

Where can I check prices and service availability?

Prices are on consultation. Please confirm the service, price and available time directly with the salon before making plans.

Are the photographs and reviews shown here real client work?

Only approved photographs and supplied reviews will be published as client work. Any empty image spaces are clearly marked as placeholders.
